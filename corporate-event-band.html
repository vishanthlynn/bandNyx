Corporate event band

Band Nyx performs live music for corporate annual days, office celebrations and private company events. The band is based in Vijayawada and travels for suitable bookings in its verified service cities.

Suitable corporate events

  • Annual days and employee gatherings
  • Brand or venue events that need a live set
  • Mixed-audience sets in Telugu, Hindi and English

What to send with a quotation request

Share the event date, city, venue, audience and whether you need a short reception set or a longer performance. Pricing is quoted for that scope. Older performances have varied in fee, and no public page states a guaranteed current rate.

Review performance footage and the repertoire approach before the call. Current technical requirements are confirmed during booking because a rider file is not published on the site.
