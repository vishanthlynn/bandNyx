About NYX

Band Nyx — six musicians. One sound built for a room that wants to be loud.

Band Nyx is a six-piece live band from Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, known for high-energy performances that blend rock, melody, and crowd interaction.

From college festivals and cultural events to private concerts and international performances, Band Nyx has built a reputation for professionalism, musical versatility, and an electrifying stage presence.
