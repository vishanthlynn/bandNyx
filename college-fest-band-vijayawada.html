College fest band in Vijayawada

Band Nyx is a six-piece live band based in Vijayawada. College cultural committees can book the band for campus festivals, cultural nights and student concerts in Andhra Pradesh.

Suitable campus events

  • College festivals and cultural nights
  • Campus concerts with a mixed student audience
  • Sets in Telugu, Hindi and English

Performance format

The lineup covers vocals, guitars, bass, drums and keys. Share the date, venue, audience size and preferred duration so the band can confirm whether the format fits the stage.

Watch existing performance footage on the live page and review the six-piece lineup. A separate technical rider file is not published; ask for current setup requirements when you request a quotation.

How a college books the band

  1. Call, WhatsApp or send the booking enquiry with the fest date and city.
  2. The band confirms availability and the details needed for a quotation.
